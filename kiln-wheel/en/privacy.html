Privacy Policy

Nekolab ("we") makes the mobile game Kiln Wheel ("the game"). This policy explains what the game keeps on your phone, what it sends when the online features are on, and how to delete it.

1. What we collect directly

The game has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, date of birth, phone number, address, email or location. There are no purchases and no analytics. The only ad is one rewarded ad you choose to watch (the Glaze box), see section 5. What the online leaderboard and shape gallery use is listed in section 3.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer, and delete them within a year after answering.

2. Kept on your phone only

Your fired pots, scores, streak, history and sound and vibration settings are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. If Android backup is on, they may be included in your device backup. Uninstalling the game removes them.

3. Online leaderboard and shape gallery

When "Online leaderboard" is on in Settings (it starts on), the game sends the following to our database to show today's leaderboard, other players' pots and the shape gallery. It sends no name or contact details. Turn it off and the game keeps working offline and sends nothing more.

The database is a service of Supabase, Inc. (USA), stored on servers in the Seoul, South Korea region. Leaderboard and gallery screens show only the automatic name and the records. To stop one internet address from sending too many requests, we keep an unreadable one-way code (hash) of the address and delete it within 3 hours. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

4. Deleting your data

In the game, Settings > Delete my data removes everything on your phone and deletes your online name with all its runs, likes, shapes, votes and reports from the server at once. If you are offline at that moment, the deletion is sent the next time the game connects. You can also email us with the name and number shown in Settings; when both match, we delete that name and all its records.

5. Ads and sharing

The game has one rewarded ad that only plays when you tap its button ("Glaze box": watch it to the end for one cosmetic glaze, never anything that changes the score). Google AdMob shows it and collects directly your advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (which may be used to estimate an approximate location), in-app activity such as ad views and taps, and diagnostics (Google's disclosure: developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure). Google's processing follows Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or keep this information.

Apart from this we do not sell your information or share it with third parties, except where the law requires it.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.
